# Website Content Refinement & Implementation — PT. Sinlie Cahaya Matahari

## 1. Project Context

Continue developing the existing website project for PT. Sinlie Cahaya Matahari (SCM), an Indonesian sock manufacturing company.

The project already has an initial homepage, reusable components, a teal-yellow brand identity, and temporary dummy image assets.

Your task is to refine the existing implementation using the latest information provided by the client in `balasan.docx`.

Do not rebuild the project from scratch. First inspect the existing codebase, current components, routing, assets, styling, and dependencies. Then implement the necessary improvements directly.

The objective is to create a credible, professional manufacturing company profile that communicates SCM's business background, product flexibility, international market reach, manufacturing capabilities, and business inquiry channels.

The visual direction remains **Modern Industrial Corporate**: clean, confident, professional, and visually distinctive without excessive decoration or animation.

## 2. Source of Truth and Content Accuracy

Use the client's answers in `balasan.docx` as the primary source for company-specific information.

Apply these rules:

- Do not invent company facts, certifications, clients, production facilities, manufacturing processes, or achievements.
- Distinguish confirmed information from information that remains unverified.
- Do not turn future targets or maximum-capacity figures into claims about current operational capacity.
- Do not invent a detailed company history when the client has only requested a general description.
- Keep missing information editable and easy to update later.
- Do not expose internal notes, placeholders, or uncertainty warnings in polished public-facing copy.
- Where information is insufficient, omit the claim or use neutral wording that remains accurate.

## 3. Confirmed Company Information

Use the following information in the website.

### Business Background

- The company has been involved in the sock business since 2018.
- The legal establishment of PT. Sinlie Cahaya Matahari took place in 2023.
- The company profile should use a general company introduction rather than an invented chronological history.

Suggested English copy:

"With experience in the sock industry since 2018, PT. Sinlie Cahaya Matahari provides sock manufacturing solutions tailored to different product requirements."

Suggested Indonesian copy:

"Dengan pengalaman di industri kaos kaki sejak 2018, PT. Sinlie Cahaya Matahari menyediakan solusi manufaktur kaos kaki yang dapat disesuaikan dengan kebutuhan produk pelanggan."

Do not imply that the PT legal entity itself was established in 2018.

### Target Markets

The company serves a broad range of customer types.

The company serves both domestic and export markets. The client identifies the United States and Europe as dominant export markets, while other destinations depend on incoming orders.

Suggested English copy:

"Serving domestic and international markets, with export orders primarily reaching the United States and Europe."

Suggested Indonesian copy:

"Melayani pasar domestik dan internasional, dengan pesanan ekspor yang terutama menjangkau Amerika Serikat dan Eropa."

Do not claim the company has offices, warehouses, distributors, or permanent operations in those markets.

### Product Flexibility

The client indicates that product categories and specifications can accommodate different customer requirements.

Communicate the ability to discuss customization of relevant product specifications, materials, and packaging according to order requirements.

Do not invent an exhaustive product taxonomy or claim specific manufacturing options that have not been confirmed.

### Packaging

The client states that packaging can be customized.

Suggested English copy:

"Packaging options can be tailored to customer requirements."

Suggested Indonesian copy:

"Pilihan pengemasan dapat disesuaikan dengan kebutuhan pelanggan."

Do not invent specific packaging formats, printing options, or labeling services.

### Quality Control

The client confirms that a Quality Control (QC) function exists.

The client also refers to AQL in relation to quality standards, but detailed implementation information has not been provided.

You may communicate that the company has a QC function. Mention AQL only if appropriate for the public website and do not invent inspection procedures, acceptance thresholds, defect rates, or certifications.

## 4. Handle Production Capacity Carefully

The client refers to a maximum capacity of 1,000,000 dozen per month.

However, the answer does not establish the current actual production volume or fully clarify the operational conditions behind that maximum figure.

Therefore:

- Do not present 1,000,000 dozen per month as current production capacity.
- Do not use this figure in hero statistics, promotional banners, metadata, or marketing claims.
- Do not retain the previous 100,000 dozen per month figure as a verified current capacity without further confirmation.
- Keep the capacity field configurable in the company's content data.
- Omit the capacity metric from the public homepage until the company confirms exactly what the figure represents and approves its publication.

If a statistics section becomes visually unbalanced without this figure, redesign the section rather than inventing replacement statistics.

## 9. Bilingual Website

The client requests both Indonesian and English.

Implement a consistent bilingual experience across all public-facing sections.

Requirements:

- Add an accessible language switcher, such as `ID | EN`.
- Translate navigation labels, headings, descriptions, buttons, product labels, contact content, and footer text.
- Preserve the current page and relevant navigation context when switching languages.
- Avoid mixing Indonesian and English within the same selected language.
- Use natural, professional language appropriate for a manufacturing company serving B2B customers.
- Keep company names, approved brand names, and verified technical terms consistent.

Inspect the existing routing and installed dependencies before choosing an implementation.

If the project already uses a localization library, extend the existing setup. Otherwise, use a lightweight, maintainable localization approach compatible with the current Next.js App Router structure.

Avoid adding a large dependency solely for a simple translation dictionary if the current project does not require it.

Keep translations separate from presentation components so future content changes are straightforward.
